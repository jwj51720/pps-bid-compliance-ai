# INC-126: Historical Luna + frozen-rule composition

INC-126 makes no new model call.  It measures only the combination of the
INC-125 full-dev Luna policy and the exact `v2`, `v3`, and `v21` predicates
already frozen and submission-packaged before INC-125.  Their official-law,
metadata, source-role, all-dev coverage, and source-substring audits are
recorded in INC-034/036/040/041 and INC-123.

The composition replaces exactly those three labels for every dev record;
everything else remains byte-for-byte from INC-125.  It cannot be presented
as independent validation because the frozen predicates and dev labels share
historical discovery data.  Its purpose is to measure the coherent teacher
ceiling and identify the next Gemma transfer target without inventing a new
rule or making item-specific corrections after INC-125.
