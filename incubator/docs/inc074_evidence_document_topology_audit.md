# INC-074: Evidence Document-Topology Audit

This is a zero-cost audit of organizer-provided gold evidence.  It does not
assert that a blank evidence cell has no relevant document, and it does not
use labels to choose prompts.  Its purpose is only to identify whether a
future document-local **attention** pass has a source-provenance rationale.

The source evidence locations show two complete, nonempty-evidence families:

- `v2`, `v3`, `v7`, `v23`: every nonempty gold evidence substring is in the
  announcement (`공고문`).
- `v9`: every nonempty gold evidence substring is in the specification
  (`규격서`).

This does not authorize removing other documents for a final decision: many
other items have blank organizer evidence, and the competition requires full
document coverage.  It authorizes only a controlled second-pass comparison in
which a full-notice call remains present and an active-item attention pass sees
its provenance-supported source document.
