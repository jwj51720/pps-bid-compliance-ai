# INC-045 Result: Candidate-ID Replicated Adjudication

## Evidence transport result

The candidate-ID contract completed 143/144 calls successfully, versus
INC-042's 124/144 free-quote success rate. Every successful positive evidence
is a Python-retrieved unchanged source line. The one failure was an internally
inconsistent `v2=1, evidence_id=NONE`, proving that even schema-constrained
generation needs pair validation.

## Legal decision result: rejected

The r1 law arm is unscorable due to that failure. On r2, law context improves
the two-item cohort macro by +0.07106 (0.33566 to 0.40672), but this isolated
repeat cannot satisfy the preregistered two-repeat promotion gate.

More importantly, v4 has TP 6 in every complete arm but FP 21--23. The model
marks ordinary performance requirements, proposal-evaluation tables, and
conditions that expressly recognize public *and* private performance as
specific-institution violations. Therefore this is not a candidate-ID problem
and must not be repaired through prompt cosmetics, post-hoc thresholding, or a
dev-wide escalation.

## Decision

Preserve candidate IDs as the preferred evidence transport for a future
source-grounded LLM experiment. Reject v4 active legal adjudication as tested.
Move to an independent product/procurement structural family rather than tuning
the same v4 wording.
